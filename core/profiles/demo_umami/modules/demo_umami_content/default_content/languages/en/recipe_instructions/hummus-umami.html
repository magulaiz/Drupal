1. Drain and reserve the water of a 400g tin of chickpeas.
  2. Add 3 cloves of garlic, the juice of 2 lemons, the chickpea water, and 1.5 tablespoons of tahini into a food processor and blend until smooth.
  3. Now add the chickpeas and a pinch of salt to the food processor and blend until you have the desired consistency. If your food processor has a pulse setting, use this to more easily control the blending. If the hummus is too thick then add a teaspoon of cold water, blend, and repeat if necessary until you have just the right texture.
  4. Spoon the hummus into a bowl and drizzle the surface with olive oil. Sprinkle a little paprika across the top.
  5. If you want to make the hummus look even more special, roast a few chickpeas and scatter across the top.
  6. To serve, toast some pitta bread and drizzle the bread with a little olive oil. Sprinkle with a little za'atar if you are able to find some, or fresh finely chopped oregano.
